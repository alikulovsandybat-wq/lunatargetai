FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need any technical skills to use Luna Ads? ▼

No experience needed. Luna Ads guides you step by step through a simple Telegram chat — just answer a few questions and your ad is ready to launch.

Which platforms can I advertise on? ▼

Luna Ads supports Facebook and Instagram ads — both are managed through the Facebook Marketing API from a single interface inside Telegram.

How much does Luna Ads cost? ▼

Open the bot in Telegram to see current pricing plans. Ad spend goes directly to Facebook — Luna Ads charges only a small service fee.

Is my Facebook account safe? ▼

Yes. Luna Ads uses official Facebook API access tokens. We never see or store your password. You can revoke access from your Facebook settings at any time.

What budget do I need to start? ▼

You can start with as little as $1–$5 per day. Luna Ads works with any budget — from small businesses to large-scale campaigns.

Can I use Luna Ads from any country? ▼

Yes! Luna Ads works worldwide. You can run ads targeting audiences in Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Russia, Europe, the USA, or anywhere else Facebook operates.

How quickly can I launch my first ad? ▼

Most users launch their first campaign in under 5 minutes. Just open the bot, connect Facebook, fill in a few fields, and hit launch.

Your Privacy Matters

Luna Ads only accesses the Facebook permissions you explicitly grant. We do not sell, share, or misuse your data. You can revoke access at any time.
